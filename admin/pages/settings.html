Configuration

Recipients and provider credentials live in powerflow-backend/.env. Restart the backend after changing them.

  • ADMIN_ALERT_EMAIL - admin inbox
  • ADMIN_ALERT_PHONE - phone in +250... format
  • AFRICASTALKING_USERNAME and AFRICASTALKING_API_KEY - SMS
  • AFRICASTALKING_SENDER_ID - optional approved sender
  • ADMIN_ALERT_EMAIL_ENABLED / ADMIN_ALERT_SMS_ENABLED
